
This workshop is primarily intended for graduate students, postdocs and early career scientists, although all are invited to attend.  Participants are expected to be comfortable using PHOEBE (setting parameter values, manipulating constraints, running forward models, and setting up basic fitting runs) and have a Mac or Linux machine with PHOEBE 2.4 installed.

# Registration
The cost of registration is $200. Limited financial support is available to help offset registration and accommodation costs. Applicants should indicated their interest in applying for support during registration.

Those interested in taking part in the PHOEBE Workshop are invited to fill out the registration form, which can be found [here](https://forms.gle/KzWcGzA6wvA5SFvj8).
